# Clone Intelligence

Clone relationships are directional claims between a clone/inspired fragrance and an original reference. Relationship type, similarity, performance differences, price difference, notes, and provenance must be stored independently; similarity alone is not proof of inspiration.

Published results should distinguish verified evidence from editorial or algorithmic assessment and include confidence. Names and trademarks identify third-party products only; ScentGraph does not imply affiliation. Review, dispute, correction, and source-retention processes are required before commercial publication.
