# Clone Intelligence

Clone relationships connect a supplier oil item, a possible original commercial fragrance, and an optional retailer product. They are directional assessments, not automatic claims. The schema stores relationship type, similarity confidence, difference summary, performance notes, provenance, and review status independently.

All algorithmic clone scores begin as `estimated` and remain unpublished until reviewed. Similarity alone is not evidence of inspiration. Published results must distinguish verified evidence from editorial or model assessment. Third-party names and trademarks identify products only; AromaTwin does not imply affiliation. Review, dispute, correction, and source-retention processes are required before commercial publication.
