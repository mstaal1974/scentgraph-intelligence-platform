# AI and Human Review Policy

AI output is a proposal, never evidence. Generated descriptions must be original, labelled as AI-generated, checked for unsupported claims and accidental source imitation, and approved by a named human reviewer. Notes, accords, vectors, performance estimates, and clone scores retain confidence and review state.

Web verification is limited to sources whose terms and permissions support the intended use. Store source identity, URL, licence status, factual scope, and check time. Do not ingest protected prose, images, ratings, reviews, comments, article bodies, or UGC. `verified` language is reserved for `approved_for_catalogue` records.
