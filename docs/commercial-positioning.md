# Commercial Positioning

AromaTwin is an independent commercial technology asset for clone, inspired-by, and private-label fragrance retailers. Its value is not a republished third-party catalogue. It comes from supplier availability, governed entity resolution, reviewed original enrichment, proprietary scent vectors, clone assessments, provenance, and explainable matching.

The platform can later be licensed as a metered API, retailer SaaS workspace, catalogue-enrichment service, or white-label recommendation widget. Tenant controls and licensing tiers will be added without introducing retailer-specific forks.
