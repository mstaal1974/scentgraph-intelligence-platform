# Candidate Matching Workflow

Candidate matching is a reversible research step between supplier availability and the commercial catalogue. Normalised supplier names may be compared with licensed, official, manually entered, or reference-only identifiers using exact or fuzzy methods.

Each candidate records source type/reference, method, confidence, notes, and review status. A reference-only candidate remains in `match_candidates`; it cannot directly create or approve a fragrance. Human verification against an official or otherwise commercially permitted source is required before independent enrichment.
