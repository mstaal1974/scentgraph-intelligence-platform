# Taxonomy

Notes represent ingredients or perceptual descriptors and are classified as top, middle, base, or general. Fragrance-note links add pyramid position, order, and strength. Accords describe higher-level olfactory impressions and carry a per-fragrance weight.

Canonical slugs are stable machine identifiers. Aliases retain supplier spellings and translations. Merges require curator review, redirect history, provenance preservation, and a taxonomy version. Vector dimensions form a separate controlled vocabulary with scores from 0 to 1.
