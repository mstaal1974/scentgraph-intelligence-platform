# Provenance, Confidence, and Intellectual Property

Supplier files establish availability, not public fragrance identity. Official or licensed sources support independently verified facts. Restricted and non-commercial datasets remain reference-only unless documented permission changes their permitted use.

Every source records factual-reference, matching, commercial-use, text-copying, and image-copying permissions separately. Source links document purpose and confidence. Enrichment events record who generated, attached, readied, approved, or rejected a profile and when. Confidence never overrides licensing.

AromaTwin generates original deterministic descriptions from identifiers and source metadata. It does not ingest or paraphrase third-party descriptions, editorial material, reviews, ratings, comments, images, article text, or UGC. Supplier prices, codes, and commercial terms remain outside profile and enrichment outputs.

AromaTwin's defensible value comes from entity resolution, original reviewed content, taxonomy, scent vectors, clone intelligence, recommendations, validation, and audit history. Enrichment approval remains separate from public catalogue promotion.


Profile drafts preserve the supplier-item and match-candidate identifiers, bounded source confidence, and an original deterministic generation method. Reference-only candidates remain discovery hypotheses, are confidence-capped, and cannot pass approval without separate commercially permitted provenance. Draft output intentionally omits supplier prices, quantities, confidential codes, and commercial terms.
