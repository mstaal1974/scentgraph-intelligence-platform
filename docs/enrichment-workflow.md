# Independent Enrichment Workflow

Approved commercial records are independently produced rather than copied or paraphrased from restricted datasets. A reviewer records an official or permitted source URL, factual identifiers, source confidence, and check time. AromaTwin may generate an original description, note/accord proposals, and a proprietary scent vector, all explicitly marked AI-generated and `needs_verification`.

Approval requires confirmation that the description is original, sources permit the recorded use, required fields have provenance, and the status transition is allowed. Only `approved_for_catalogue` records may be published as verified. Reviews, ratings, images, article bodies, comments, and other UGC are never imported through this workflow.
